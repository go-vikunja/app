# Roadmap

Steps 1 to 46 and 47a to 47f are built, except the ones listed below: the fixes from the code review of 2026-11-23 (47g to 47i, the owner picks their order), the release itself (37d), the four planned after 1.0 and the two dropped ones; 19 is built but still in review until the iOS device test. The [decision log](decisions.md) holds the decisions that still apply, by topic. This page lists only what is left. The full plan, with every step, how the steps were reviewed, the milestones and the dated notes on each change, stays in git: `git show c2bf10d:docs/roadmap.md`. After the move the rows after 1.0 become GitHub issues, and this file can go.

| # | Step | Goal | Size | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 36d | Keyboard, mouse and trackpad | Shortcuts on every platform from one table, ⌘ on Apple and Ctrl elsewhere ([Identity and scope](decisions.md#identity-and-scope), amended): new task, search, tabs 1–4, refresh, done, edit, delete, save, send, help; a global key layer below the app lock; Esc closes the top sheet, dialog, menu or pane, leaves a page through its discard guard, does nothing at a tab root and stands down while text is composed; a visible focus ring; keyboard moves on the board and in lists; the editor page's keys; Android's Meta+/ shortcut list; immediate drag with a mouse or trackpad and trackpad pinch on the timeline. | 39a | Voice capture with Siri | "Hey Siri, add a task in Vikunja" on iOS: an App Intent with an App Shortcut phrase in English and German, also in Shortcuts, Spotlight and on the Action button. Siri asks for the title, as a phrase can hold only fixed values such as a project, never free text; projects offered to Siri as entities, so that "add a task to *Shopping* in Vikunja" works too. The task goes into the App Group like a widget's Done and reaches the outbox at the app's next run, without a token in an extension, as for the widgets ([Notifications and widgets](decisions.md#notifications-and-widgets)). To decide when the step starts: whether the intent resolves quick-add dates such as "tomorrow" itself when it runs, as the parser is in Dart, or opens the app with the text. The newer Siri that acts inside other apps works through the same App Intents | M | planned for release 1.3 (owner, 2026-11-12) |
| 39b | Voice capture with Gemini | "Add a task in Vikunja" through Gemini on Android: a create-task function through Android's AppFunctions (Android 16 and later). In November 2026 Gemini called AppFunctions only for selected partner apps, in a preview. No Google Assistant App Actions, as Gemini replaces the Assistant | M | planned for release 1.3 (owner, 2026-11-12), on hold until Gemini calls the AppFunctions of every app |
| 34 | Time tracking (licensed) | Timers and time entries when licensed | M | planned for release 1.4 (owner, 2026-11-12), with a licence or a test instance from the Vikunja maintainers ([Identity and scope](decisions.md#identity-and-scope)) |
