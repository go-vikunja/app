Rurana is an unofficial app for Vikunja, the open-source task manager you can run on your own server. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Vikunja project.

Sign in to your Vikunja server (version 2.3 or newer) — your own, your organisation's or a hosted one — or try the public demo. Rurana keeps a copy of your account on your iPhone or iPad, so your projects and tasks are there even without a connection.

Works offline
• Every screen reads from the copy on your device.
• Tasks you create, edit, tick off or move on a board while offline are sent as soon as the server answers again.
• The app syncs when it starts, while it is open and in the background.

Your tasks at a glance
• Overview: your open tasks, grouped into Overdue, Today, Next 7 days, Later and No due date.
• Upcoming: the tasks due in a date range, day by day.
• Projects with subprojects, favourites, saved filters and archived projects.
• Each project in its List, Kanban, Table and Gantt views.

Everything about a task
• Due, start and end dates, priority, progress, colour, repeat and reminders.
• Labels, assignees, subtasks and related tasks.
• Descriptions in the web's own editor, with checklists, links and tables, offline too.
• Comments with @mentions, and reactions.
• Attachments: files, photos or a new photo from the camera.
• Quick Add Magic: type a task with its project, labels, people, priority and date in one line, the way the web reads it.

Reminders and widgets
• Notifications at each reminder and at the due time, planned on the device: mark the task done right there, or be reminded again in ten minutes or an hour.
• Widgets for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen: Today, the next days, Overdue, Favourites, a project, a label or a saved filter; tick tasks off right on the Home Screen. A second widget adds a new task.

Also
• Several accounts, on one server or on several, and guest access through a shared link.
• Labels, teams, and projects shared with people and teams.
• Your account settings, the two-factor status and the sessions signed in to your account.
• On an iPad in landscape, and on a 13-inch iPad in portrait too, a task opens beside the list.
• Light and dark mode, British English and German.
• For self-hosted setups: trust your server's own certificate or CA certificate, use a client certificate or access headers, and allow plain http only after you confirm it.

Privacy
• An optional app lock with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode; task titles can be hidden in notifications and widgets.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting. Rurana talks only to the servers you add. The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.
