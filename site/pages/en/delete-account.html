Deleting your account

Rurana has no accounts of its own. Your account is on the Vikunja server you signed in to, and that server holds your data. I, the developer of Rurana, hold none of it.

In the app

  1. On the Account tab, open Account settings.
  2. At the bottom, tap Delete account. Rurana explains what happens and opens your server's page for deleting your account in the browser.
  3. Confirm there. If your account has a password, the page asks for it.
  4. Your server emails you a link to confirm the deletion. Three days after you confirm, it deletes your account. Until then you can change your mind: the Account tab shows the date with “Cancel deletion”.

Without the app, you can do the same on the web: open the settings in your server's Vikunja web app and delete your account there.

What is deleted

Vikunja deletes your account together with all projects and tasks you own; what you shared with others passes to them. Whether the server keeps anything beyond that, for example in its backups, and for how long, depends on whoever runs it; ask them.

To remove the account from your device as well, sign out of it on the Account tab, as described below.

If your server does not allow it

Some servers do not allow deleting accounts in the app; Rurana then tells you so. On a server that does not send emails, the confirmation never arrives. In both cases, ask whoever runs the server to delete your account.

The public demo

Please do not delete the shared account of the public demo on try.vikunja.io: other visitors use it, and the confirmation email goes to an address you cannot read. The demo server is reset at irregular intervals.

Removing an account only from this device

To remove an account from Rurana but keep it on the server, tap Sign out at the bottom of the Account tab (Sign out of this account if you have several). Your other accounts are listed under Accounts on the Account tab; open the menu beside one and choose Remove. As a guest of a shared link, tap Remove guest access. Rurana then deletes that account's copy of your data, its comment drafts, its widgets' content, its notifications and its tokens from the device, as the privacy policy describes.
