Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to {{contact_email_link}}.

It helps if you tell me:

  • the version of Rurana, shown at the bottom of the Account tab;
  • your device and its version of Android or iOS;
  • the version of your Vikunja server, shown on the Account tab under Server;
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.

Never send passwords, tokens or other secrets.

Connecting to your server

  1. Open Rurana and enter your server's address: the address you open Vikunja with in your browser, for example https://vikunja.example.com.
  2. Tap Continue. Rurana checks the server and shows what it offers.
  3. Tap “Continue to sign-in” and sign in on your server's page in the browser. Rurana then downloads your projects and tasks.

Rurana needs Vikunja 2.3 or newer. If your server sits behind an access proxy or uses certificates of its own, you can set that up in the server's connection settings in the app.

The public demo

To look around without a server of your own, tap “Try the public demo” on the first screen. Rurana connects to try.vikunja.io, a server run by the Vikunja project. Sign in with the shared account: username “demo”, password “demo”. Everyone uses this account and the server is reset at irregular intervals, so do not enter personal data there.

Reporting a bug

Email me as described above. {{issues}}

Report security problems privately: by email or in a confidential issue, never in a public one.

Rurana and Vikunja

Rurana is an unofficial app. For questions about your account or your server, ask whoever runs the server. Everything about Vikunja itself is on vikunja.io.
