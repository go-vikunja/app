Rurana

Your tasks from your own Vikunja server, on Android and iOS.

Rurana is a free app for Vikunja, the to-do app you host yourself. It keeps a copy of your projects and tasks on your device, so you can work offline, and sends your changes once you are back online. It reminds you of your tasks, shows them in widgets and handles several accounts and servers.

Your data

Rurana has no server of its own and contains no analytics and no advertising: your tasks go only to your Vikunja server. The privacy policy has the details.
