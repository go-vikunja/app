# Name exploration v2

A second look for a name for the unofficial Vikunja mobile client. The current working name is **Taskuna**. It is kept below as the baseline to compare against.

Earlier research and rejected names are in [`../README.md`](../README.md#alternatives): Viku/Vicu, Kuna, Cria, anything with "Vikunja", Llama/Vicuna/Alpaca/Guanaco, Puna, Chaku, Kunja and Vicudo.

**Constraints:**
- At most 10 characters.
- Easy to say and spell in English and German.
- Points to Vikunja (camelid, Andes), to tasks, or ideally to both.
- Does not sound official.
- Friendly and modern.
- Works as `<Name> – client for Vikunja`.

## Recommendation

**Rurana** is the only new name that beats Taskuna.
- It is Quechua, the language "vicuña" comes from, and it means roughly "what is to be done". So it is literally a to-do.
- It does not share *-kuna* with the Kuna app.
- No conflicts were found.

The catch is that the meaning has to be explained. Taskuna explains itself.

If the story does not appeal, keep Taskuna. The other names are either weaker or have conflicts.

## Final ranking

| # | Name | Meaning | Pros / cons | Conflict check (2026-10-09) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rurana** (6) | Quechua *rurana*, the *-na* form of *ruray* "to do": "that which is to be done". The same pattern gives *mikhuna* "food" ("what is to be eaten"). | **Pro:** a real word from vicuña's language that means "to-do"; flows like Taskuna (EN "roo-RAH-nuh", DE "Ru-ra-na"); unique; no *-kuna* or *Viku-*. **Con:** the meaning needs a line in the store text; it says less about tasks than Taskuna does; Spanish and Portuguese ears hear *rana* "frog" at the end, which is harmless and even cute; Cusco Quechua spells the verb *ruway*. | **Clean.** No app in the App Store (US, DE) or on Google Play. No brand, company or trademark in web results. No bad meaning in EN, DE, ES or PT. |
| 2 | **Taskuna** (7, baseline) | *task* + *vicuña* | **Pro:** explains itself (task app + vicuña); easy in EN and DE. **Con:** shares *-kuna* with the Kuna iOS client; reads as a made-up blend. | **Clean.** No exact match in either store. The closest is "Taskula" on Play (developer Cherry102). |
| 3 | **Yupana** (6) | The Inca counting board, from Quechua *yupay* "to count" | **Pro:** an authentic Andean tool for keeping track of things; friendly and brandable (EN "yoo-PAH-nuh", DE "Ju-pa-na"); its story pairs well with the check-llama logo. **Con:** counting is not quite tasks; English speakers may read "Yup-ana". | **Medium risk.** No app in either store. However, **Yupana** is a German financial-reporting SaaS for the DACH region (HGB balance sheets and liquidity forecasts, listed on Capterra, G2 and OMR). It is software sold in the same market, so check trademarks before using this name. There was also an Ecuadorian student math app (2017). |
| 4 | **Woolist** (7) | *wool* + *list*, a nod to vicuña wool | **Pro:** "list" says what the app does; instantly readable in English; no apps with the name. **Con:** wool makes people think of sheep, not camelids, so the link to Vikunja is weak; German and Spanish speakers may stumble on the "oo"; it sounds generic and close to "Woolies" (Woolworths). | **Low risk.** No app in either store. There is a UK Etsy wool shop called "TheWoolist" (different goods). Play results show Woolworths apps. |
| 5 | **Lamalist** (8) | *Lama* (the German spelling of llama) + *list* | **Pro:** the animal and the list are both plain to see. **Con:** close in sound to **lemlist** (a well-known French sales-email SaaS, which has a Play app); near the English word *Lamaist*; carries the "Llama" AI association; in German you can read it as *Lama-List*, "llama's trick"; clunky. | **Medium risk.** No exact app. "Llama Lists" (travel app) is close, and so is lemlist. |

**Store titles for the winner:**
- Store listing: `Rurana – client for Vikunja` (de: `Rurana – Client für Vikunja`).
- OAuth scheme: `vikunja-rurana`, which still meets the D14 prefix rule.
- Possible store line: *"Rurana" is Quechua for "what is to be done"*. Have a Quechua speaker confirm the wording before release.

## Raw ideas (40)

**Quechua and Andean words**
1. **Rurana**: "what is to be done". Shortlisted.
2. **Ruray**: "to do". There is an app called "Ruray" on Play (Fernando Apaza).
3. **Tukuy**: "to finish" or "all". Hard to spell from hearing ("Tu-kui").
4. **Yupana**: the Inca counting board. Shortlisted.
5. **Quipu / Khipu / Kipu**: Inca knotted-cord records, a perfect match for lists. **However, "Quipu for Vikunja" (Alexandre Vazquez, App Store, July 2026) is another Vikunja client**, so add it to the README's "Names not to use". Khipu is also a Chilean payments company, Kipu a US health-records company and a bookmark app. Also EN "KEE-poo".
6. **Minka**: communal work. Shortlisted.
7. **Ayni**: reciprocity. Shortlisted.
8. **Ayllu**: the Andean community unit. An app with the name exists, and it is hard to say ("EYE-yoo").
9. **Chaski**: the Inca relay runner, a nice metaphor for sync. Many apps already use it (Chaski, Chaski Tracker, CHASKi.fit, delivery apps).
10. **Pacha**: "world, time". Pacha is a club brand (Ibiza, Barcelona) and has apps.
11. **Tambo**: an Inca waystation. Also a Peruvian store chain with apps.
12. **Inti / Killa**: sun and moon. Crowded, and *Killa* sounds like "killer".
13. **Kusi**: "joy". KUSI is a San Diego TV station, and Finnish *kusi* means "piss".
14. **Ichu**: the grass vicuñas graze on. It sounds like a sneeze ("achoo", German "hatschi").
15. **Wari**: a pre-Inca culture. Many apps (wa/ri, SendWari).
16. **Lamkana**: from *llamk'ay* "to work", and it looks like *Lama*. Its form depends on the dialect, and it is clunky.
17. **Altiplan**: Altiplano + plan. The App Store (DE) has a business app called "Altiplan" (EG).
18. **Tikuna**: tick + vicuña. It is the name of an Amazonian indigenous people and ends in *-kuna*. Do not use.

**Wool and fibre**

19. **Woolist**: wool + list. Shortlisted.
20. **Vuna**: Croatian for wool. Shortlisted.
21. **Lana / Lanita**: "wool" (ES/IT). A common first name; in Mexican slang *lana* means "money".
22. **Tuft**: Tuft Pro, Tuft for pet parents and others already exist.
23. **Skein**: hard for Germans to say; knitting apps use it.
24. **Ruana**: an Andean wool poncho. No link to tasks.
25. **Chullo**: an Andean knitted hat. Everyone gets the "CHOO-yo" pronunciation wrong.
26. **Woolly**: "Woolly for Mastodon" exists.

**Herding**

27. **Herdly**: shortlisted.
28. **Flock**: an existing team-chat app.
29. **Roundup / Corral**: Roundup is a herbicide trademark.
30. **Tropa**: "herd" (ES). Reads as "troop".

**To-do wordplay and blends**

31. **Lamalist**: shortlisted.
32. **Taskama**: task + lama. ES/PT read it as *tas-cama* ("bed"), and it echoes *Kama*.
33. **Listuna**: list + vicuña. It reads as "list tuna".
34. **Tickuna / Checkuna**: the same *-kuna* problem as Taskuna, only stronger.
35. **Doluna**: do + *-una*. It reads as *luna* (moon), with no camelid in it.
36. **Tadaa**: crowded and has no camelid.

**Invented or short**

37. **Kunja / Kuni**: too close to Kuna.
38. **Lamita**: "little llama" (ES). Cute, but no link to tasks.
39. **Paqo**: Quechua *paqu*, "alpaca". It is also the word for an Andean shaman and reads as "Paco".
40. **Vicky**: a person's name, and it starts with *Vic-*.

## Method and limits

- **App Store:** the Apple iTunes Search API, `entity=software`, US and DE stores, on 2026-10-09. Some queries hit the rate limit and were repeated.
- **Google Play:** the web search at `play.google.com/store/search?c=apps` (en-US). Play shows fuzzy results, so "none" means no app has the same or a nearly identical name.
- **Web:** web searches for the name plus app, brand and trademark terms, plus dictionary checks for the meanings.
- **No formal trademark search** (EUIPO, DPMA, USPTO). The TMview API did not respond, and Justia's search is behind a bot check. Do a formal search for the chosen name before the first store release. For Yupana, do it now.
